Why balancing matters

The highest cell decides when charging must slow down.

In a parallel battery bank, average pack voltage can look normal while one cell reaches the upper limit early. That cell can force CCL reduction, interrupt charging, and leave capacity unused in every other cell and pack.

This controller watches each battery independently. It qualifies spread over eight unique samples, selects one pack deterministically, and adjusts aggregate charge allowance until the selected pack receives approximately 2 A - the condition used to support the battery's own passive balancing behavior.

01

Recover usable headroom

Reducing cell spread can delay the moment when the highest cell reaches the BMS charge limit, allowing the bank to approach full charge more uniformly.

02

Reduce protection cycling

Qualified entry and exit rules avoid reacting to one noisy sample while addressing persistent imbalance before repeated high-cell events dominate charging.

03

Lower top-of-charge stress

Once balancing is complete, the learned float can reduce unnecessary time at elevated cell voltage, a recognized contributor to LiFePO4 calendar-aging stress.

Dynamic float voltage

Learn the pack at the crossing. Do not hold it higher than necessary.

The system buffers four pack-voltage samples before the first configurable Vmax crossing, includes the crossing sample, and captures four qualifying samples afterward.

Vfloat = (1 / 9) · Σ Vpack[k],   k = -4 ... +4

After full-SOC balancing completion, the learned value becomes a CVL candidate. The final voltage remains the lowest of the learned float, Dyness CVL, enabled Cerbo UI CVL, and the 56.5 V hard ceiling.

Lifetime claim boundary: lower top-of-charge voltage can reduce voltage-related aging stress, but actual service life also depends on temperature, depth of discharge, current, storage SOC, cell quality, and operating time. This project does not claim a quantified lifetime extension.

Implementation, not a black box

Every layer has one job and one authority.

01

RS485 truth

CID2 0x42 supplies addressed cells, current, voltage, and capacity. CID2 0x44 supplies status. Master 0x61 and 0x63 supply system SOC, extrema, limits, and permissions.

02

Cell 16 estimator

A trimmed common voltage plus per-battery filtered offset removes pack-quantization stair steps. Correctly decoded global extrema remain final physical bounds.

03

Qualified control

Eight samples qualify entry and low-spread exit. Feed-forward estimates parallel current share; a slow PI corrects selected-pack current without assuming equal sharing.

04

Guardian continuity

Instance 101 remains selected while the worker restarts. Missing or stale telemetry produces a persistent, charge-capable 54 V / 20 A / 100 A fallback.

Power-safe architecture

The controller may restart. The selected BMS must remain.

The worker, guardian, and Node-RED run independently. Routine deployments restart only the component that changed. The guardian preserves the D-Bus identity, last valid SOC, fallback limits, and live VE.Bus measurements when available.

Frequently asked questions

Dyness balancing and dynamic float, answered.

How are normal-mode SOC charge-current limits calculated?

The editable defaults are 15 A per battery at 97%, 6 A per battery at 98%, and 3 A per battery at 99% or above. The controller multiplies the selected value by the unique confirmed battery inventory, so three batteries produce aggregate limits of 45 A, 18 A, and 9 A. These caps are bypassed during balancing, and lower Dyness BMS or Cerbo UI limits remain authoritative.

Why balance parallel Dyness PowerBrick PRO batteries?

Cell and pack differences can make the highest cell reach the BMS limit before the rest of the bank is full. Controlled balancing can reduce spread, avoid premature charge limiting, improve usable capacity, and reduce repeated high-cell protection events.

How does the controller decide when to balance?

Each battery needs eight consecutive unique valid samples with spread strictly above 30 mV, charge MOSFET ON, protection clear, and valid independent capacity SOC. Exactly 30 mV does not qualify. Qualified packs are selected in deterministic address order.

How can a reduced float voltage support LiFePO4 lifetime?

After balancing, holding a battery near the top-of-charge voltage longer than necessary can add voltage-related calendar-aging stress. The learned float follows the actual pack voltage around the configured high-cell crossing. It can reduce that exposure, but no fixed lifetime extension is promised.

When is the learned float voltage applied?

Only after full-SOC balancing completion while master CID2 0x61 SOC is 100%. It remains a candidate, not an override: Dyness CVL, enabled Cerbo UI CVL, and the 56.5 V hard ceiling can only reduce it. SOC at or below 98% clears the learned value.

Is the 3.500 V/cell float trigger editable?

Yes. The management page accepts 3.000 through 3.650 V/cell, with 3.500 V as the default. Changing it discards the old learned float so samples from two thresholds cannot be mixed.

How is Cell 16 calculated?

Cells 1-15 form a trimmed common voltage. The same battery's pack-voltage subtraction provides a noisy relative observation, filtered independently per address. Fresh CID2 0x61 global extrema bound the result; an extremum is assigned exactly only when both its battery and cell ID identify Cell 16.

Which telemetry provides per-battery SOC?

The per-battery integer and interpolated SOC come from verified extended 24-bit capacity fields in CID2 0x42. CID2 0x44 is status-only. Master CID2 0x61 remains authoritative for system SOC, guardian SOC, DVCC, and global charge derating.

What happens if RS485 communication fails?

The persistent guardian remains connected and advertises 54.0 V CVL, 20.0 A CCL, and 100.0 A DCL with charge and discharge enabled. It retains the last valid SOC and waits for two complete fresh worker samples before returning to NORMAL.

Does this bypass Dyness protection or send battery commands?

No. RS485 operation is read-only. Dyness limits, permission bits, MOSFET states, and physical protection remain authoritative. The project does not transmit undocumented battery control commands.

What DIP switch setting and baud rate are required?

The documented PowerBrick PRO setting is 00110, read in the order printed on the battery. It selects the Victron and others profile at 115200 baud, 8N1. Always verify switch numbering and orientation on the battery label.
